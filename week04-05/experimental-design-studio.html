Experimental Design Studio

Four hands-on stations that turn the unit's principles into practice: grade a hypothesis, sort variables from confounders, audit the Séralini study, and pressure-test a design of your own.

The through-line. Every station points back to one real, retracted study — Séralini et al. on GM maize and Roundup — the cautionary tale behind why design choices decide whether a result can be believed.

Grade a hypothesisPick one. See how it scores against four marks of a good hypothesis — then a stronger rewrite.

Sort the variablesScenario: does high chronic stress lower influenza-vaccine antibody titre at 4 weeks? Assign every factor.

Anything that isn't the variable you change (IV) or measure (DV), and isn't held constant, is a confounder waiting to happen.

Audit the Séralini designFor each design decision, judge it: sound or a red flag? Then reveal the verdict.

Build & pressure-test a designMirror the worksheet. Fill what you can, then get a completeness & rigour check — coaching, not grading.
